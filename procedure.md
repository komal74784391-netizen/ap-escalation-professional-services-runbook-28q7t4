# Escalation Procedure

1. **Confirm the open balance.** Recompute the invoice value less settled amount from
   the settlement ledger before contacting the supplier.
2. **Classify the discrepancy.** Short payment, duplicate charge, disputed unit price,
   damaged goods deduction, or missing credit note.
3. **Collect evidence.** Statement, purchase order, goods receipt, and the supplier's
   remittance advice for the quarter.
4. **Open a case record.** Record the document reference, supplier, category, amount in
   dispute, and the assigned purchasing lead.
5. **Raise the supplier notice.** Use the notice template in `templates/escalation-notice.md`;
   the notice must quote the document reference and the open balance.
6. **Log the response.** Suppliers have ten business days to answer; escalate to the
   category board if unresolved at the deadline.
